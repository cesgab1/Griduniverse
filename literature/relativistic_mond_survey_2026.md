# Which relativistic MOND theories survive? (survey, Sep 2026; three independent literature searches)

Tests: Solar System (alpha1/alpha2, Cassini quadrupole), CMB + P(k), lensing = dynamics, GW speed = c, stability.
P passes, F fails, U untested, (c) claimed analytically only.
