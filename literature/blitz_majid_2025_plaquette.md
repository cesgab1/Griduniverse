# Blitz & Majid, "Quantum curvature fluctuations and the cosmological constant in a single plaquette quantum gravity model"
Class. Quantum Grav. (2025), doi:10.1088/1361-6382/ada90c ; arXiv:2405.18397

Model: quantum Riemannian (noncommutative) geometry on ONE square graph, 4 vertices, 2D Lorentzian.
Metric = square-lengths assigned to edges (spacelike horizontal, timelike vertical). Path integral over edge lengths
with UV cutoff eps and IR cutoff L.

Results
- <R_av> -> 0 as L -> inf; fluctuations Delta R ~ (3/8 pi^-1/4) G^{3/4} eps^{-1/2} L^{-5/4} (eq 17): UV-divergent, survive renormalization (eq 21)
- With Planck UV / 100 TeV IR cutoffs: rho_QG ~ 1e39 GeV^4 >> rho_SM ~ 1e8 GeV^4 -> supports Carlip / Wang-Unruh:
  huge vacuum fluctuations make space oscillate at tiny scales, leaving little net expansion
- Adding a bare cosmological constant makes the fluctuation magnitude non-smooth (eq 28) -> authors read this as a hint
  that a bare Lambda should vanish

Limits (authors' own): 2D, single cell, qualitative; no Hilbert space; Lambda result only to first order; graph fixed.

Use for Griduniverse
1. Language: "metric = lengths on edges of a graph" is exactly our links; Majid's framework is a rigorous way to write the grid.
2. Qualitative support: bare Lambda disfavoured, dark energy as a residue of fluctuations -> consistent with our dynamic,
   fluctuation-derived dark energy (rho ~ adot^-1/2, beat Lambda in the AeST fits).
3. Not usable as numbers: cutoff-dependent, 2D, no cosmological-scale prediction. L is a cap on edge size, not an averaging region.
