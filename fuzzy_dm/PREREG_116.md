# PREREG 116 -- is the missing pull a galaxy-sized quantum wave? (fuzzy dark matter vs the smallest galaxies)
Committed BEFORE running iter116_fuzzy.py.
Idea: dark matter = an ultra-light boson; its quantum wave sets the smallest possible dark core. A self-gravitating
ground state ('soliton') of mass M has half-mass radius r = 3.93 hbar^2/(G M m^2) (standard boson-star result, memory).
A dwarf with velocity spread sigma and half-light radius R_e holds M_1/2 = 3 sigma^2 r_1/2 / G inside r_1/2 = 4/3 R_e
(Wolf+2010). Its dark matter cannot be more compact than the wave allows -> m >= sqrt(3.93/3) hbar/(sigma r_1/2).
If dwarf cores ARE solitons, sigma x r_1/2 is the same for all (= 1.14 hbar/m): one particle mass for all.
Data: ALL Milky Way dwarfs with a measured (not upper-limit) velocity spread in Simon 2019 (ARAA 57, 375;
arXiv:1901.05465) Table 1 -- population, no picking. Read via browser; checksum-verified like iteration 111.
Outputs: per-dwarf minimum mass; the strongest; how many dwarfs exceed 1e-21, 1e-20, 1e-19 eV; spread of sigma x r_1/2.
Compare: Lyman-alpha forest (~2e-21 eV, memory), star-heating in ultra-faints (Dalal & Kravtsov 2022, ~3e-19, memory),
the ~1e-22 eV that fuzzy-DM papers use to give Fornax-size cores (memory).
Free choices: Wolf estimator (1), coefficient 3.93 (1), resolved-sigma cut (1). Look-elsewhere: the strongest of N dwarfs
is a maximum -> reported with its own error and the 2nd/3rd strongest.
Expectations: strongest bound ~1e-20 eV (Segue 1-class); several dwarfs above 2e-21; sigma x r_1/2 spread > x30 -> NOT one
soliton mass for all; the masses that give Fornax-size cores (~1e-22) are excluded by the smallest dwarfs by >x30.
