# Fate of the universe under the grid dark-energy law (`fate.*`)
rho_DE ~ adot^-beta (beta = 1/2; fit 0.63 +/- 0.22). Far future: a ~ t^((2+beta)/beta) = t^5. It accelerates forever, but the
expansion rate H = 5/t keeps falling (Lambda: H constant).
- No shrinking, ever: to turn around, adot must reach 0, where the grid tension rho_DE ~ adot^-beta becomes infinite.
  The slower the grid stretches, the harder it pushes, so the law prevents its own collapse. A closed universe
  (Omega_k = -0.01, -0.05) also never turns around. Only a breakdown of the law itself (beta < 0, excluded at ~3 sigma by the
  fits, or the links snapping/saturating) could change this.
- Cooling: the CMB is 0.075 K at 1e11 yr, 8e-6 K at 1e12 yr, 1e-15 K at 1e14 yr, 1e-45 K at 1e20 yr. The horizon's own
  temperature (hbar H / 2 pi k_B) falls with it: 1e-30 K now -> 2e-33 K at 1e14 yr -> 2e-59 K at 1e40 yr. It approaches absolute
  zero but never reaches it.
- Difference from Lambda: Lambda has a permanent floor at 2.2e-30 K (de Sitter temperature) and a fixed horizon; the grid law has
  no floor and its horizon keeps growing (proper size ~ t/4).
- Structure stops growing (99% frozen) at ~60 Gyr (Lambda: ~46 Gyr). After that the only heat sources are local: stars until
  ~1e14 yr, then black holes ending as Planck stars (bounce 3e26 yr stellar, 5e37 yr Sgr A*, 5e45 yr largest; black_holes/).
  In the fountain picture, heated fluid in wells stops cycling once infall stops (~60 Gyr).
