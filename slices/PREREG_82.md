# Pre-registration, iteration 82: dark energy measured slice by slice (Oct 4 2026, written BEFORE running)
Purpose for quantum gravity: the pattern rho_DE(z) is the large-scale OUTPUT a micro theory must produce.
  smooth rise-and-fade (Claim 1)  -> dark energy follows a cosmic clock (a 'now'-dependent term, f(K, tau))
  random slice-to-slice jumps      -> fluctuating dark energy (causal-set 'everpresent Lambda', Sorkin)
  flat                             -> a plain constant (unimodular integration constant)
Method: rho_DE(z)/rho_DE(today) at nodes z = 0.4, 0.8, 1.4, 2.5 (4 free numbers), straight lines between nodes, value 1 at z = 0,
held at the z = 2.5 value beyond. Plus Om, h, omega_b. Flat space. Data: DESI DR2 BAO + CMB distance priors + supernovae
(Pantheon+, DES-Dovekie, Union3). Errors from the curvature of chi2 at the best fit.
Comparisons: chi2 of the constant pattern (all 1) and of the Claim 1 pattern (computed at the same nodes) against the measured
values with their covariance (4 numbers each).
Expectations: values within ~+/-10% of 1 at z = 0.4-0.8 with errors ~5-8%; poorly measured at 2.5 (error >= 20%); a mild excess
(~1.03-1.08) at z = 0.4-0.8. Both patterns within ~2 sigma; Claim 1 closer than constant. No sign of random jumps beyond errors
(which would only weakly constrain everpresent Lambda since its jumps are random).
