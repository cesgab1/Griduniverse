# PREREG 117 -- is the extra pull AROUND the galaxy or IN it? (Coalesce: 'the effect would be around it, not in it')
Committed BEFORE running iter117_local.py.
Physics: Poisson's equation is local -- div g = -4 pi G rho_here. Material OUTSIDE a region (any shape: shells, rings,
repulsive or attractive) satisfies div g = 0 inside it and adds NO inferred local density. Round systems: shell theorem.
Flat disks: an outside ring can push stars inward in the plane, but only by pushing them outward vertically by the same
amount -- the local density inferred from radial + vertical forces together is unchanged.
Test at the Sun (all inputs verified this session):
 measured total surface density within |z| < 1.1 kpc: 68 +/- 4 Msun/pc^2 (Bovy & Rix 2013, defined as integral of rho dz)
 visible (stars, remnants, gas) within 1.1 kpc: 43.8 (McKee+2015; error scaled from 47.1 +/- 3.4) -- version 1
 visible per Bovy & Rix themselves: stars+remnants 38 +/- 4, plus gas 13.7 +/- 1.6 (McKee) -- version 2
 cross-check: McKee+2015 local dark density 0.013 +/- 0.003 Msun/pc^3 (not independent).
'Around, not in' predicts excess = 0. Free choices: which visible estimate (2, both reported).
Expectation: excess 16-24 Msun/pc^2, 2.5-5 sigma above zero -> unseen mass sits IN the galaxy at the Sun's position.
