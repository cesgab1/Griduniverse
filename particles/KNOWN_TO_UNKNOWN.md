# 'Dark' and 'anti' things, ranked from most known to least known (Oct 5 2026)
| rank | thing | how it entered physics | evidence today | invented to account for something? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Antimatter | fell out of Dirac's equation (1928) | made, trapped, measured; mirrors matter to 1e-12 | No -- a prediction of existing math, confirmed 1932 |
| 2 | Neutrinos | Pauli 1930, to save energy conservation in beta decay | detected 1956; masses from oscillations 1998 | YES at first -- then confirmed directly |
| 3 | Higgs field/boson | 1964, to explain how particles get mass | discovered 2012 (LHC) | YES at first -- then confirmed directly |
| 4 | Quantum vacuum activity | predicted by quantum field theory | measured (Lamb shift, Casimir force); its GRAVITY is wrong by ~1e120 | No -- a prediction; its weight is the mystery |
| 5 | Dark matter | Zwicky 1933 (clusters), Rubin 1970s (galaxy rotation), to explain missing gravity | its gravity seen many independent ways (rotation, lensing, Bullet Cluster, CMB); never detected as a particle | YES -- inferred from gravity; identity unknown |
| 6 | Dark energy | 1998, to explain the accelerating expansion | amount measured to ~1%; behaviour hinted (DESI); nature unknown | YES -- largely a label for 'whatever accelerates the expansion' |
| 7 | Inflation | 1980, to explain why the universe is so flat and uniform | several predictions confirmed (flatness, tilt n_s = 0.965); key signal (primordial gravitational waves) not seen | YES -- invented to solve puzzles; partly supported |
| 8 | Gravitons | required if gravity is quantum | never detected; may be practically undetectable | YES -- needed by theory, untested |
| 9 | Axions, sterile neutrinos, dark photons... | to fix other puzzles (strong-CP problem, anomalies) or as dark-matter candidates | none detected; many ruled out in parts of their range | YES |
| 10 | Our Planck grains / tension patches | this project, to account for black-hole entropy and dark energy's behaviour | indirect only (beta ~ 0.6, line count, stretching patches); no direct test | YES |
Pattern: some 'invented to account for' ideas were later confirmed directly (neutrinos, Higgs); dark matter and dark energy are
still only seen through their effects on gravity and expansion.
