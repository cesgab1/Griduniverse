# The Light-Cone Model (Coalesce, Oct 4 2026): Special + General Relativity as one intuitive picture, no grid

Rule: use only what SR and GR say about space and time; add something only where GR stops (time zero, overall shape).

## Postulates
L1 (SR) Everything began at ONE EVENT. The universe is the inside of that event's future light cone; nothing is outside it.
L2 (SR) 'Now' = the surface of equal elapsed time (proper time) since that event, for observers moving with the cosmic flow.
         These surfaces are hyperboloids: the same 'age' and the same expansion rate everywhere on them.
L3 (SR) Those 'now' surfaces curve the OPEN way (negatively curved space).
L4 (GR) Matter and energy bend space-time inside the cone (Einstein's equations); far from the event the picture is ordinary
         cosmology; near it, GR's infinite density is where L1 replaces 'a point of infinite density' with 'one event'.
L5 (SR) Energy of empty space must look the same to every observer -> its pressure is minus its density (w = -1): the baseline
         dark energy is a constant. A departure is only possible if it depends on the one thing every observer agrees on in
         this picture: the cosmic 'now' of L2 (elapsed time / expansion rate on the equal-time surface).

## What it explains at once
- Time zero: an event (a light-cone tip), not a place.
- The cosmic rest frame / CMB frame: the frame of the equal-time surfaces (L2).
- Why dark energy is (nearly) constant: SR (L5). Why it may drift: it can follow the cosmic 'now' -- Claim 1 (dark energy follows
  the expansion rate on the equal-time surface) is allowed without any grid.
- Shape: open (L3).

## Predictions testable with today's data
P1 space is curved the open way: Omega_k > 0 (DESI DR2 + CMB: +0.0023 +/- 0.0011, ~2 sigma today; next releases decide).
P2 dark energy = constant, or follows the cosmic 'now' (Claim 1: Delta chi2 -5.4/-7.2/-6.8 vs constant; DESI 2027 decides).

## Open (not answered by SR + GR)
- Dark energy's SIZE: still a free number in this picture.
- Finite or infinite: L3 gives open curvature; finiteness would be an extra assumption (compact open space) -- not added.
- Near the event the real universe is hot and dense, not empty: L1 is the causal skeleton, GR supplies the content.
