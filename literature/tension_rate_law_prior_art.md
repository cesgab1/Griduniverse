# Prior-art check: the Tension-Rate Law (rho_DE ~ adot^(-1/2); d ln rho_DE / d ln a = q/2) — 3 Oct 2026
Quick web search (not exhaustive). No exact match found. Closest relatives, all different:
- Running vacuum (rho = c0 + nu H^2), holographic / Ricci dark energy (rho ~ H^2, H-dot): depend on H or curvature,
  increase with H; ours depends on adot = aH with a negative power and needs no free constant.
- Sorkin's 'everpresent Lambda' (rho ~ 1/sqrt(spacetime volume), random-walk origin): same sqrt(N) fluctuation logic as our
  motivation (beta_derivation.py), different counting variable; its simplest form is ruled out (our earlier test).
- Phantom-crossing models (quintom, coupled dark sector, F(R), 'SM+GR condensate' arXiv:2607.08120): cross w = -1 at
  other redshifts (e.g. z ~ 1.5) or with free parameters; none found that pins the crossing exactly to q = 0.
- 'Kinematic phantom crossing' (arXiv:2609.19602) means w0wa-type parametrisations, not an adot law.
- Parametric reconstructions of q(z) exist, but as fitting forms, not a dark-energy law tied to q.
Status for the paper: claim as 'to our knowledge new'; cite Sorkin, running vacuum, holographic/Ricci DE as relatives;
before submission run an ADS full-text search for '\dot{a}^{-1/2}', 'rho_DE propto q', 'phantom crossing at transition'.
