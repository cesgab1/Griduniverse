# PREREG 100 -- does the 'when' matter? Fit each era separately (Coalesce)
Committed BEFORE running iter100_eras.py.
Idea: supernovae weight the recent universe, BAO reaches further back. If timescape's growth-of-voids timeline is right, the
void fraction today (f_v0) fitted in each era must agree. Same check for LCDM's matter share Om as a CONTROL (it should agree).
Eras: supernovae z < 0.2 / 0.2-0.5 / 0.5+ (each slice its own magnitude offset, so shape only);
BAO z < 1 (4 bins) / z > 1 (3 bins), ruler r_d H0 free in each.
Errors from delta chi2 = 1. Three supernova sets.
Expectations:
- Control: LCDM Om agrees across eras within 2 sigma.
- Timescape: f_v0 drifts with era (later eras want more void) by > 2 sigma in at least two of three SN sets.
If timescape's eras agree as well as LCDM's, the SN-BAO mismatch of iteration 99 is not about 'when'.
