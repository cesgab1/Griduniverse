# PREREG 126: anti-readers -- same capacity, opposite interpretation?
Committed before collecting data and before code.

Idea (Coalesce): antiparticles act as null/opposite on the plane; different capacity -> slightly different behaviour.
Three parts:
(a) CAPACITY: if anti-readers had different capacity, masses would differ. Collect best particle/antiparticle mass
    (or q/m) comparisons (neutral kaons, proton/antiproton, electron/positron). Expectation: equal to 1e-11..1e-18.
(b) BEHAVIOUR vs CAPACITY: collect measured CP asymmetries (particle vs antiparticle decay-rate differences) in kaons,
    D mesons, B mesons, Bs mesons, Lambda_b baryons. Test: does the asymmetry size rise or fall monotonically with the
    particle's mass (capacity)? Spearman rank correlation, 5-6 systems; significance threshold p < 0.05 two-sided
    before claiming a trend. Expectation: no trend (Standard Model sets them by mixing phases, not mass).
    Caveat in advance: different observables are not strictly comparable; we use the largest well-measured asymmetry
    per system (free choice, 1) and also report a second choice.
(c) BUDGET: is the measured particle/antiparticle difference big enough to leave the observed matter excess?
    Compute the standard dimensionless CP measure J * (product of quark mass-squared differences) / T^12 at
    T ~ 100 GeV and compare with the measured baryon-to-photon ratio ~6e-10. Expectation: short by ~1e10
    -> a real gap, i.e. extra particle/antiparticle difference is needed somewhere (an observation, not a made-up entity).
