  lambda - 1 (G_cosmo/G_N, our N_eff estimate)    : bound 2e-02 -> needs N >~ 5.9e+01 (c = 1)   inside window
  alpha (pulsars / solar-system preferred frame)  : bound 1e-07 -> needs N >~ 1.0e+07 (c = 1)   inside window
  beta' (graviton speed, GW170817)                : bound 1e-15 -> needs N >~ 1.0e+15 (c = 1)   inside window
