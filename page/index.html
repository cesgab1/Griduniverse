Method: Planck units with core scale ℓ = ℓ_P. Temperature from the surface gravity of the outer horizon, T = (1 − 4Mℓ²/r₊³)/(8πM) for the capped core and 1/(8πM) for GR; brightness from blackbody emission over the horizon area (photon-like, which reproduces the textbook 1/(15360πM²)). Including every particle species speeds all three up by a similar factor without changing their shapes. Smooth evaporation ends when a typical Hawking quantum (energy ≈ kT) would carry more than the mass left above the floor: M = 0.20 m_P for GR, 1.3029 m_P for the capped core. The capped core's temperature falls to zero at M_min = 1.2990 m_P, so evaporation slows to a halt instead of running away.

If the seed-and-snap ending is right

  • Nothing in nature gets hotter than about 2.5 × 10³⁰ K through black-hole evaporation, and every black hole, whatever its size, ends with the same 2.55 GJ flash.
  • Hawking radiation stays thermal if the information goes into the seed. That contradicts recent "Page curve" calculations, which have the information coming back out, so the two can't both be right.
  • Planck remnants can't be dark matter, because none are left. Finding Planck-mass relics would favour the remnant ending instead.
  • Our own universe could be a seed, grown from a Planck-density de Sitter core. Such seeds start out closed, which predicts slightly positive spatial curvature (Ω_k just below zero). Current data allow it: Ω_k = 0.0007 ± 0.0019.

Tested against public data

The same evaporation law applied to a real primordial black hole, one that formed with about 5 × 10¹¹ kg and is finishing now. Time runs to the right as time remaining shrinks. The telescopes that search for these explosions watch the last 0.2 to 100 seconds. The three endings split only when the temperature passes about 10¹⁷ GeV, in the last 10⁻³⁹ seconds.

Full derivation behind the dial's numbers: Schwarzschild to river form to the Buchdahl limit

Proposition. Outside a static, spherical, non-rotating body of mass M and radius R, the surface river speed, surface clock rate and outgoing-light headway depend only on the compactness C = GM/Rc², and all three reach their horizon values together at C = ½. Inside a uniform-density star, the centre clock stops and the central pressure diverges together at C = 4/9, which is the forbidden gap.

  1. Start from Schwarzschild ds² = −(1 − r_s/r) c²dt² + dr²/(1 − r_s/r) + r²dΩ², r_s = 2GM/c² Birkhoff's theorem: this is the unique vacuum solution outside any spherical mass, star or black hole.
  2. Change time coordinate (Gullstrand–Painlevé) dT = dt + √(r_s/r) / [c(1 − r_s/r)] dr ds² = −c²dT² + (dr + v dT)² + r²dΩ², v(r) = c√(r_s/r) = √(2GM/r) Substitute and expand: the dr² and dt dr terms reorganize into a flat space with a flow v. Nothing physical has changed.
  3. Evaluate the river at the surface v(R)/c = √(r_s/R) = √(2C) Also equals the Newtonian escape speed √(2GM/R), since ½v² = GM/r.
  4. Static clock on the surface (dr = 0, dΩ = 0) dτ = √(−ds²)/c = √(1 − v²/c²) dT = √(1 − 2C) dT Holding still means moving at v against the flow: this is special-relativistic slowing with the river speed.

  6. Horizon condition v = c ⇔ r = r_s ⇔ C = ½ ⇒ rate = 0, headway = 0 All three quantities hit their limits at the same compactness, which proves the "one dial" claim.
  7. The river speed equals the escape speed. The clock rate is the Schwarzschild factor for a clock held at rest on the surface, which is the same as special-relativistic slowing for swimming against the river.

About "light drag"

In the river picture, outgoing light moves at c through the flowing space, so its progress is c minus the river speed. Anyone measuring light locally still gets exactly c. At a horizon the headway is zero and light stalls.

The forbidden gap

4/9 < C < 1/2

No static star can be more compact than 4/9 (the Buchdahl limit). Objects in the shaded gap can't hold themselves up, so a star can't creep smoothly into a black hole. It sits below 4/9 and then collapses.
