Verdict by the pre-registered rule (break needs both treatments, >= 2 regions, both conventions): NO BREAK.
Brown-dwarf disks continue the stars' line -- one recipe. Agrees with the published conclusions.
Expectations: slope 1.3-2.0 and brown dwarfs on/slightly above the line: HIT. 'Convention B brings them closer':
MISS -- B makes faint objects' disks COLDER, so their inferred dust goes UP (+0.5-0.9 dex), moving them further
above the line. Upper Sco (older) brown dwarfs: median 0.5-1.2 Earth masses of dust depending on convention.

LESSON 129: the same recipe (disk ~ mass^1.7-1.9) runs from Sun-like stars down to failed stars of 0.02 Msun.
The dust-temperature CONVENTION shifts brown-dwarf dust masses by ~0.5-0.9 dex -- as large as any effect we could
test -- so at the low end the convention, not the data, is the main uncertainty. Carry to 130: failed stars look
like small stars; expect them to be born by cloud collapse down to the fragmentation floor.
