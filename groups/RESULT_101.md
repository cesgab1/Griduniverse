# RESULT 101 -- does the galaxy rule get stronger in deeper wells? (groups + deepest galaxies)
Only visible stars/gas, measured speeds, galaxy rule. Outputs: iter101_groups.txt.
Depth law tuned so clusters get x5.3 (exponent 0.327 from the median galaxy depth).
| system | depth v^2 (km/s)^2 | boost predicted | boost measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| median SPARC galaxy | 1.2e4 | 1 (anchor) | 1 |
| galaxy groups (56, Milgrom 2019) | 3.3e4 | 1.38 | 0.72-1.20 (stellar M/L_K 0.6-1.0) |

| clusters (Coma) | 2e6 | 5.3 (tuned) | 5.3 |
Reading: both checks come in BELOW the depth law (~1-1.5 sigma each), pointing to flat-then-jump rather than a smooth rise.
Not decisive: stellar mass-to-light uncertainty and small numbers. Not blind (Milgrom's median seen first). A web-tool transcription of
the group table failed a spot check and was discarded; only the paper's stated median is used.
Decider: systems at depth 1e5-1e6 (hot-gas groups and poor clusters, sigma 300-600 km/s), stars AND hot gas counted. The depth law
predicts x2.5-4 there. (Published MOND analyses of X-ray groups report shortfalls of roughly x2 -- from memory, unverified.)
Scorecard: expectation 'groups ~1, depth law mildly disfavoured' MET; 'any rise must be a step' -- suggested, not established.
