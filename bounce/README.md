# Did the universe start with a Big Bang? Grid bounce tests (bounce_running.*)

Two different claims:
- The hot, dense early phase (CMB, light elements, expansion): well established. Our fits use it. Not in question.
- A beginning at a singularity (t = 0): open. A Planck-scale grid caps the density at about one Planck mass per cell. As for
  black holes (Planck stars), the natural replacement is a bounce: an earlier contracting universe compresses to the cap and
  rebounds. Effective law (loop quantum cosmology): H^2 = (8 pi G/3) rho (1 - rho/rho_c), rho_c ~ 0.4 Planck density.

Constraints from our own results:
- Our dark-energy law forbids a turnaround (rho_DE ~ adot^-1/2 diverges as adot -> 0; future/). So no cycles: at most ONE
  bounce, from a contracting branch that was always contracting. That branch mirrors ours: dark-energy dominated when large,
  then dust, then radiation, then the bounce.

Test 1: bounce INSTEAD of inflation (the contracting phase makes the primordial spectrum; the matter/LambdaCDM-bounce family,
Cai & Wilson-Ewing 2015). The red tilt n_s = 0.965 comes from modes leaving the horizon while dark energy is still ~0.3% of the
contraction (w_eff ~ -0.003). Because that fraction falls steeply toward the bounce, the running is fixed once the tilt is:
| dark energy in the contracting branch | DE fraction at exit | running alpha_s | Planck 2018 (-0.0045 +/- 0.0067) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lambda | 0.0029 | +0.213 | 32 sigma off |
| our grid law (rho_DE ~ |adot|^-1/2) | 0.0029 | +0.230 | 35 sigma off |
Leading-order estimate (n_s - 1 = 12 w_eff at sound-horizon exit, constant dust sound speed), evaluated exactly on the
background. Cai & Wilson-Ewing note the running is positive but leave its size open; given the tilt and the scaling of the
dark-energy fraction, it is ~+0.2 (~72 delta). Literature (LQC review arXiv:2301.10215) adds: matter bounces typically give
too large a tensor-to-scalar ratio unless tuned, which then brings non-Gaussianity and anisotropy problems.
-> FAILS: a bounce that replaces inflation doesn't fit the measured spectrum (unless the dust sound speed varies just right,
which would be a picked function, not a derivation).

Test 2: bounce + inflation (standard LQC). The bounce replaces the singularity; inflation still makes the spectrum. Signatures
appear only on the largest scales if inflation lasted near its minimum (~60-65 e-folds): extra or suppressed power and a
broken consistency relation at l <~ 30. The review reports these can ease the large-scale CMB anomalies (low power,
hemispherical asymmetry, A_L). Not yet computed with our model, which has no inflaton. Adding one is a new ingredient, not a
derivation from the grid.

Status: 'no singularity' is natural on a grid and consistent with everything we have. 'No inflation' (a bounce doing
inflation's job) fails the running test by ~30 sigma. What survives is bounce + inflation, which our model doesn't yet explain.
