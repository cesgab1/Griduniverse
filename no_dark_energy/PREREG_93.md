Committed BEFORE running iter93_bare.py.

## Ingredients
Ordinary matter (baryons; omega_b = 0.0224 fixed by deuterium/BBN), photons, neutrinos. Nothing dark.
Lessons kept (none involve dark energy/matter):
- L1 cells widen, not inserted (iter 84): ordinary expansion; no new term.
- L2 light does not fade (iter 91): no dimming correction allowed for supernovae.
- L3 gravity strength fixed (Eot-Wash, iter 87): G constant.
- L4 space measured nearly flat: Omega_k = +0.002 +/- 0.0013 (iter 81); used as a CHECK, not an input.
Lessons dropped: Claim 1 law, horizon container, grain-count dark energy, dark link (all dark-energy related).

## Two versions (2 free choices)
- BARE-OPEN: Omega_b = 0.0224/h^2 (~5%), curvature fills the rest. Free: h.
- BARE-FLAT: flat, so atoms must be 100% of critical. Free: h. (Then omega_b is a prediction, compared with BBN.)

## Observables (6) and expectations
1. Supernova shape (3 sets): BARE-OPEN worse than LCDM by delta chi2 10-60 (like iter 92 OPEN at Om->0.05); BARE-FLAT +270..+930 (= EdS).
2. BAO with sound horizon integrated directly (no dark-matter fitting formula), calibrated once to LCDM r_d = 147.09 Mpc: BARE-OPEN fails by > 10 sigma.
3. CMB acoustic angle l_A (geometric part only; peak heights not computed): BARE-OPEN off by > 100 sigma (open space shrinks angles strongly).
4. Age at measured H0 67.4 / 73: BARE-OPEN ~ 13-14.5 Gyr -> age gap CLOSES (expected success).
5. Flatness: BARE-OPEN needs Omega_k ~ 0.95 vs measured 0.002 -> fails by > 500 sigma. BARE-FLAT needs omega_b ~ 0.45 vs BBN 0.0222 +/- 0.0005 -> 20x too many atoms.
6. Structure: growth of baryon lumps from recombination to today. Expected growth factor ~ 30-100 in BARE-OPEN, versus ~1e4 needed (seeds ~1e-4, today ~1). Gap > 100x: no galaxies.
Also listed qualitatively (not computed): flat galaxy rotation curves, cluster lensing, CMB third peak -- all known to need dark matter.
