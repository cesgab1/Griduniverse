## D5 'vacuum energy gravitates' -> unimodular gravity (Einstein 1919; Weinberg 1989; Ellis 2011, memory)
Keep only the part of Einstein's equations that does not see a constant energy: vacuum energy then cannot bend
space-time at all, and Lambda reappears as a free constant of integration, fixed once by the universe (measured).
Same predictions for every classical observation -> consistent with all data; the 1e120 mismatch stops being a
prediction (Lambda's VALUE is still unexplained). Grid link: unimodular gravity fixes the space-time volume element =
a fixed count of cells per volume -- what a grid does naturally. Sorkin's causal-set estimate of Lambda's size
(~1/sqrt(number of cells)) lives in exactly this framework. Candidate 'better equation' for the project.
Test: classical tests cannot tell it apart; quantum-gravity differences debated -> brainstorm.

## D6 energy conditions -> singularity without assumptions?
Hawking-Penrose 'the universe began at a point' assumes the strong energy condition (density + 3 x pressure >= 0).
Dark energy (and inflation) violate it, so those theorems do not apply to our universe. The replacement theorem
(Borde-Guth-Vilenkin 2003, memory) needs NO energy condition but only covers universes that have ALWAYS been expanding
on average; a contraction-then-bounce (our grid's density cap) escapes it. Better statement: 'any universe that only
ever expanded had a beginning; a bounce is not excluded by any theorem.' Bounce signatures were tested in Family 6.
