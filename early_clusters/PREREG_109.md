# PREREG 109 -- how surprising is a 2e13 Msun protocluster at z ~ 5.7 (JADES-ID1, Chandra/Webb, Jan 2026)?
Committed BEFORE running iter109_rarity.py.
Data (press release, Chandra 2026): JADES-ID1, z ~ 5.7 (~1 Gyr after the Big Bang), total mass ~2e13 Msun, >= 66 members, hot X-ray gas.
Method: standard cosmology (Planck 2018 parameters) with cold dark matter; CAMB linear power; Sheth-Tormen halo abundance; expected
number of halos above M in the JADES fields (assumed ~175 arcmin^2 total, disclosed) for 5.2 < z < 6.2. Also: number expected with
NO dark matter (ordinary matter only cannot grow lumps after recombination fast enough; computed via the growth argument).
Expectations:
- With cold dark matter: expected number of >= 2e13 halos in that volume ~1e-3 to 1e-2 -> a real tension (~3 sigma) unless the mass
  is overestimated by ~2-3x (masses of protoclusters at this epoch are uncertain by factors of a few).
- Without dark matter: essentially zero (lumps too small, iteration 93).
- Lesson expected: early massive clusters require MORE early pull, not less -> they argue for the extra pull existing early.
