# Pre-registration, iteration 50 (committed BEFORE running): growth of structure with the frame field's horizon-scale effect

Iteration 48 found that the frame field (which carries Claim 1) changes gravity only for horizon-sized ripples. Calculation:
VCDM linear perturbations (BORROWED, De Felice, Mukohyama & Pookkillath, PLB 816, 136201, 2021): all equations as in LCDM
(Newtonian gauge, Phi = Psi, dust continuity and Euler) EXCEPT the momentum constraint, which becomes
   Phi' + aH Phi = (3 a^2 / 2 k^2) rho_m theta * F(k),   F(k) = [k^2 - 3 a^2 dH/dt] / [k^2 + (9/2) a^2 rho_m]
(rho in units 3 M_P^2, H^2 = rho_total). F = 1 exactly when the dark energy has w = -1 (check). Claim 1 background (E4).
Runs: A = our model (Claim 1 background + VCDM constraint); B = same background, ordinary constraint (F = 1, what all our fits
implicitly assumed); C = LCDM. Observables: comoving matter density at z = 0 versus scale k; decay of the potential (late ISW).
Expectations:
 P1 F = 1 to machine precision on the LCDM background (code check).
 P2 A/B differs from 1 only on near-horizon scales: |A/B - 1| < 1e-3 for k >= 0.01 h/Mpc; a few percent at k ~ 3e-4 h/Mpc.
    Sign: today w > -1 (F > 1) but for most of the history w < -1 (F < 1); net sign not predicted.
 P3 the effect sits where cosmic variance is largest (few modes): NOT testable with galaxy surveys; at most a few-percent change
    of the late ISW on the largest scales, also cosmic-variance limited. If P3 holds, the horizon-scale effect is a consistency
    check passed, not a new prediction.
