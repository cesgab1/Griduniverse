Committed before running iter97_spin.py. Target: the extra pull clusters need, x5 (Coma) in mass.

v_rot 200 km/s, disc scale 5 kpc):

   mass estimates by ~x1.3-2 and averages out over many clusters (lensing and X-ray agree). Expect: cannot give x5 on average.

   relative to ordinary gravity ~ (v/c)^2 ~ 1e-5. Expect: all < 1e-4 of what is needed.
Expected verdict: spin and angle are real and measurable but 4+ orders of magnitude too small (angle: at most x2 per cluster).
