lambda: what the data allow and what a naive grid gives

(1) N_eff = 2.99 +/- 0.07 (CMB + BAO + abundances): G_cosmo/G_N - 1 = -0.0073 +/- 0.0094
    2-sigma: G_cosmo/G_N - 1 in [-0.026, +0.012]  ->  lambda - 1 in [-0.008, +0.017]
    (treats a change of G as extra radiation: exact in the radiation era, approximate at the CMB; published BBN bound
     |G_cosmo/G_N - 1| <~ 1/8 -> 0 <~ lambda - 1 <~ 0.1, or <~ 0.01 when the other preferred-frame parameters vanish)
(2) naive grid (hard preferred-frame cutoff, vacuum energy of modes responding to stretching): lambda = -0.50 -- NOT 1.
    A non-covariant cutoff gives non-covariant induced terms (the known 'Lorentz violation percolates through loops' problem).
