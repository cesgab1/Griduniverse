Verdict: the non-spherical emergent gravity we could build (published covariant version, and our local Verlinde variant)
fails the Bullet Cluster by 5.2-5.9 sigma, like the galaxy rule. Reason is the same: its extra pull is generated by
visible matter, ~90% of which is the gas, so the deepest curvature lands on the gas; lensing puts it on the galaxies.
The feature that helped in Coma (extra pull tracks spread-out gas) is exactly what hurts here.

lag the matter -- a time-delay version is NOT tested and is the only escape left inside this family); B's centre is a
free choice. A1 = the covariant theory's curl-free approximation; A2 confirms it.
