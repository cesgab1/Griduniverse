# RESULT 117 -- the unseen mass is IN the Milky Way at the Sun, not only around it
Measured surface density within 1.1 kpc of the plane 68 +/- 4 Msun/pc^2 (Bovy & Rix 2013) vs visible:
 - McKee+2015 visible 43.8 -> unseen 24 +/- 5 -> 4.7 sigma above zero
 - Bovy&Rix stars + McKee gas 51.7 -> unseen 16 +/- 6 -> 2.8 sigma
 - cross-check (same physics): local dark density 0.013 +/- 0.003 -> 4.3 sigma; ~29 Msun/pc^2 over the column.
Expectation 16-24, 2.5-5 sigma -- HIT. 'Around, not in' predicts 0 -- disfavoured at 2.8-4.7 sigma (depends on the visible
estimate; the two differ mainly in the star count, 33 vs 38).
Meaning: the local law of gravity reads only mass that is HERE; any arrangement outside (shells, rings, repulsive or not)
adds nothing to it. At our own position in the galaxy there is ~16-24 Msun/pc^2 of mass we cannot see (about 1/3 of the
total). Same conclusion as clusters (Bullet: the extra sits on the galaxies) -- now at the Sun.
Caveats: the dynamical value comes from Bovy & Rix's model fits to stellar motions (Newtonian gravity); in a modified-gravity
world the 'unseen' column would be phantom, not matter -- that alternative is tested elsewhere (iterations 102, 114).
