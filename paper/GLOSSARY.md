# Grid Universe: names for every piece (use these consistently in the paper)

Each entry gives the name, what it is in plain words, what it corresponds to in standard physics, and its status:
**NEW** (our claim, testable), **BORROWED** (standard physics we adopt or reproduce), **PICTURE** (a way of describing,
no separate test), **OPEN** (not yet settled between the authors).

## 1. The structure

| Name | Plain meaning | Standard-physics counterpart | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Grid** | Space itself, built as a network of cells under tension | space-time (in Khronon theory: a metric plus a preferred slicing) | PICTURE |
| **Grid cell** (or **Planck cell**) | The smallest unit of space: a many-sided polyhedron, on average 15.5 faces (Fig. 10) | none: the continuum has no cells | PICTURE; size limits below |
| **Node** | The centre point of a cell | a lattice site | PICTURE |
| **Wall** | The flat face shared by two neighbouring cells | a Voronoi face | PICTURE |
| **Tension link** | The connection from one node to its neighbour through their shared wall; carries tension; strength set by the wall's area divided by the link's length | the discretised field equation (graph Laplacian with Voronoi weights) | computed (Fig. 2) |
| **The Mosaic** | The random arrangement of cells; no two alike, no preferred direction | isotropy (a regular lattice would imprint its axes, ~20%) | computed |
| **Cell scale** | The average cell size. Max 5.7e-28 m (measured: gamma-ray-burst timing). Min: the Planck length 1.6e-35 m (model, set by the density cap) | the Planck length | max: tested; min: model |
| **Time layer** | All of space at one moment; moments follow layer after layer | Khronon's preferred time slicing | **OPEN** (layers as moments in time, or as sheets in space; see Fig. 0 caption) |
| **The Ocean** | The fluid that fills the gaps between layers; has mass, does not shine or collide | cold dark matter | BORROWED (behaves exactly as cold dark matter) |

## 2. What the grid does

| Name | Plain meaning | Standard-physics counterpart | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Draw-in** | A mass pulls the surrounding grid toward itself from every side, fading as 1/distance² | the gravitational field (Newton) | computed on the random grid (slope −1.96 vs −2) |
| **Tick rate** | How fast the grid's clock runs at a place; slower near mass. Paths bend toward slower ticking: that is falling | gravitational time dilation (GR); measured (Pound-Rebka, GPS) | BORROWED (Khronon reproduces GR, Solar System tests) |
| **Grid tension** (or **cosmic tension**) | The overall stretch of the whole grid; it pushes space apart | dark energy | the IDENTIFICATION is ours; its behaviour is Claim 1 |
| **Stretch rate** | How fast the grid is being stretched at a given time | the expansion rate ȧ (related to the Hubble rate) | standard |
| **Grid speed limit** | The fastest anything can move: one cell per tick of the grid's clock. Light is a pure ripple of the grid and travels at it; matter is a pattern that must keep its own ticking, so it can only approach it | c = ℓ_P/t_P exactly; also c² = (link tension c⁴/G)/(mass per length c²/G), like a wave on a string | PICTURE (explains why c is universal, not its value in m/s; light shows no grid effect down to 5.7e-28 m) |
| **Density cap** | The most the grid can be compressed: about 0.4 of the Planck density | loop-quantum-cosmology bounce density | BORROWED, consistent, not testable now |
| **Bounce** | A collapse that hits the density cap and rebounds instead of forming a singularity | LQC bounce | PICTURE; a bounce alone FAILS (needs inflation, ~30σ) |
| **Planck star** | The dense core inside a black hole, held at the density cap | Rovelli-Vidotto Planck star | consistent; no observable effect for >1e26 yr |
| **Two-layer electron** | The electron as a pattern whose two handed halves sit on two layers; its mass is the leak between them | Kaplan's domain-wall fermion | BORROWED construction, computed on the grid (exactly one electron; no fake copies) |

## 4. What we reproduce but do not claim as new
Newton's law and its inverse square (the Draw-in), general relativity in the Solar System (Khronon base), light bending,
orbits (ellipses, rosettes in a Pool, chaos near binaries; ν Octantis and Pluto checked), cold dark matter on all scales
tested (CMB, clusters, Bullet Cluster, lensing), black holes. Open problem shared with standard cosmology: galaxies follow
tighter rules (Tully-Fisher, one acceleration scale) than Pools placed by abundance matching.

## 5. Naming rules for the text
- Use the bold names above every time; introduce each once with its standard-physics counterpart in brackets.
- Never call a PICTURE item evidence. Never call a BORROWED item ours.
- 'Claim 1' and 'Claim 2' are the only places the paper says 'we predict'.
