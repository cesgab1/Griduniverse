# Pre-registration, iteration 90: do dark energy and dark matter exchange energy? (Oct 5 2026, written BEFORE running)
Model (background only): d rho_DE/d ln a = beta q rho_DE - xi rho_DE ;  d rho_c/d ln a = -3 rho_c + xi rho_DE
(xi > 0: dark energy turns into dark matter; xi < 0: the reverse). Baryons untouched. The early-universe matter density used for
the sound horizon and recombination is the TRUE early value (the a^-3 coefficient at high z), not today's.
Baselines: constant dark energy (beta = 0) and Claim 1 (beta = 1/2). Data: DESI DR2 BAO + CMB priors + Pantheon+ / DES-Dovekie /
Union3. Profile chi2 over xi in [-0.4, 0.4]; others re-fitted.
Rules: 'no exchange detected' if xi = 0 lies within 2 sigma; report the 95% range as the measured limit.
Expectation: xi consistent with 0, 1-sigma width ~0.05-0.1 (background-only); a mild preference for xi < 0 (dark matter -> dark
energy) is possible because it mimics evolving dark energy with the constant baseline; with Claim 1 the preference should shrink.
Limitation: growth of structure not included (would tighten the limit); some signs can be unstable in perturbations.
