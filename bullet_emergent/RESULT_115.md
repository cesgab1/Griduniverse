# RESULT 115 -- delayed response of space (lag) in the Bullet
Scan tau = 0-400 Myr, three gas-speed assumptions (iter115_lag.txt).
Best: 4.8 sigma (tau 275 Myr, f 0.5); 4.9 (350 Myr, f 0); 5.0 (200 Myr, f 1). No delay goes below 4.8 sigma.
Expectation 'no tau < 3 sigma, best 3-5' -- HIT. Pass condition (< 2 sigma) -- not met, so no Milky Way prediction derived.
Why: a delay puts the extra pull where matter WAS. The galaxies' past positions lie behind them, toward the collision
point, so the lagged pull drifts around the centre (tip wanders 13-163 kpc from the markers) but never rides on the
galaxies; meanwhile the Newtonian part still sits on the gas. To keep the pull ON the moving galaxies, the source must keep
moving with them -- carry its own momentum -- and anything that does that behaves as unseen matter.
Caveats: straight-line kinematics, v_rel and T from memory, fitted mass split gives v_main 36 km/s (main blob dominates);
a lag is the simplest 'memory'; a field with momentum is a different theory (effectively a dark-matter fluid).
