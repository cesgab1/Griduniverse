# PREREG 98 -- trace back: were clusters made together, or did mutual gravity pull them in? (Coalesce)
Committed before running iter98_trace.py.
Method: timing argument (Kahn & Woltjer 1959): two bodies start together at the Big Bang moving APART with the expansion; gravity
alone (radial Kepler orbit, r = A(1-cos eta), t = B(eta - sin eta), A^3 = G M B^2) must turn them around and bring them to
today's separation and approach speed in 13.8 Gyr. Gives the trajectory AND the mass needed. Lambda ignored (raises mass ~10-30%).
Cases:
1. Milky Way + Andromeda: separation 770 kpc, approaching at 110 km/s (van der Marel+2012/2019). Visible ~1.5e11 Msun.
2. Virgo infall: zero-velocity radius 7.2 Mpc (galaxies there are just turning around now, Kashibadze+2020). Visible ~1e14 Msun.
Expectations:
- Both pairs started moving APART with the expansion and were turned around by gravity (not born together in place);
  MW-M31 turnaround a few Gyr ago... expected turnaround roughly 4-8 Gyr after the Big Bang.
- Mass needed: MW+M31 ~3-5e12 Msun (x20-35 visible); Virgo ~5e14 Msun without Lambda (x5 visible).
- So trajectories independently show the same missing pull as speeds and lensing.
