# RESULT 120 -- the galaxy pattern does not depend on our viewing angle
SPARC, 2765 points / 155 galaxies with tilt >= 30 deg (inclinations checksummed). Offset from the all-galaxy curve:
| tilt | points / galaxies | offset (dex) | scatter (dex) |
| 30-50 deg | 484 / 34 | -0.003 +/- 0.005 | 0.138 |
| 50-70 deg | 1342 / 70 | +0.005 +/- 0.004 | 0.146 |
| 70-90 deg (edge-on) | 939 / 51 | -0.000 +/- 0.004 | 0.137 |
Gas-dominated subsets (5-16 galaxies each): -0.04 to -0.09 dex with errors 0.02-0.09; same sign in all three tilt groups,
so it reflects gas-dominated vs star-dominated points, not viewing angle.
Against expectations: groups agree within 0.1 dex -- HIT (within 0.01 dex). Scatter larger at low tilt -- MISS (0.138,
no larger than the others) -> tilt-correction errors are not a noticeable source of scatter.
Verdict: the 2-D -> flat-disk deprojection convention does not create the galaxy pattern; a 1/sin^2 artefact would have
shifted the low-tilt group by tenths of a dex.
