# Project default methods (adopted Oct 2026 after the convention drills; Coalesce approved)
These replace conventions wherever they apply. Older iterations keep their original method; any rerun uses these.

1. Galaxy masses (from D1): lead with gas-dominated points (gas from 21-cm radio, no mass-to-light assumed). Use all
   points only as a second line, and always report mass-to-light 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8.
2. Comparing systems with the galaxy pattern (from D2): use galaxies' own measured boost curve (SPARC, binned medians),
   not an assumed interpolation formula. A formula may be shown alongside, never alone.
3. Cluster and group masses (from iteration 119): every measured radius is its own data point (total pull vs visible
   pull there). No conclusion may rest on a mass-defined boundary such as r500.
4. Beginnings (from D6): say 'a universe that only ever expanded had a beginning (Borde-Guth-Vilenkin); a bounce is
   not excluded by any theorem'. Do not cite the Hawking-Penrose singularity theorems for our universe (their energy
   condition fails with dark energy).
Cross-checks kept, not defaults: free-bin dark energy (D3), lensing vs hot-gas masses (D4). Candidate equation under
study: unimodular gravity (D5).
