# PREREG 107 -- Einstein's static universe at the Planck scale as the starting state (Coalesce)
Committed BEFORE running iter107_static.py. Calculation (numbers vs data where possible).
Setup: Einstein 1917 static closed universe (one parameter: Lambda = balance), filled with radiation; radius R.
Static balance: Lambda = 8 pi G rho / c^2 and rho = 3 c^2 / (16 pi G R^2).
Questions and expectations:
Q1 Density of a Planck-radius static universe vs the grid's cap (0.41 Planck density): expect same order (0.01-1) -- dimensional,
   not a discovery.
Q2 Can it become OUR universe? Our visible universe holds ~1e89-1e90 units of entropy. Expect a Planck-sized static universe holds
   ~1-10 -> must create ~1e89 (needs ~68 e-folds of conversion = inflation/reheating, an added ingredient).
Q3 Our dark-energy law (rho_DE ~ |adot|^-1/2) at a static phase (adot = 0): diverges -> incompatible unless cut off (expected).
Q4 Seeds (new, grid-specific): thermal jiggling in a static phase whose heat capacity follows AREA (our grid's entropy counts area)
   gives SCALE-INVARIANT seeds (string-gas mechanism, Nayeri-Brandenberger-Vafa 2006, from memory). Amplitude ~ (T/T_P)(l_P/l_cell);
   measured seeds need T ~ 1e-4 T_P with l_cell = 1.67 l_P. Expect: a STATIC EINSTEIN universe that is (a) big enough to hold our
   entropy and (b) at that temperature is impossible (balance fixes T from R) -- mismatch > 1e6.
Verdict expected: Planck-scale Einstein universe is a consistent picture of the 'door frame' but cannot by itself produce our hot,
seeded universe; the area-law thermal-seed mechanism is the one new lead, needing a static phase held by the grid itself, not by Lambda.
