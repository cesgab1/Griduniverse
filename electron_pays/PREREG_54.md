# Pre-registration, iteration 54 (committed BEFORE running): a NON-quantum answer to dark energy's size
Idea already in the model (E5, cosmology_fits/electron_energy_budget.py, gas_decoupling.py): the early electron is ~1% heavier;
when it relaxes it hands delta m_e c^2 per electron to the grid's tension. If that IS dark energy:
    rho_DE(z_s) = delta * m_e c^2 * n_e(z_s)        (then rho_DE evolves by Claim 1, beta = 1/2)
and if the switch happens when the cosmic gas loses thermal contact with the CMB (a standard-physics event, computed with
CAMB), then delta is PREDICTED from the measured dark energy -- and compared with the CMB measurement delta = 0.0099 +/- 0.0049.
Expectations:
 E1 'Compton heating rate = expansion rate' (z ~ 124): predicted delta ~0.4-0.6% (beta = 1/2), within ~1 sigma of 0.99%.
    Other decoupling criteria (gas 10% / 50% colder, halfway) give 0.05-2.5%: the choice of criterion is a free choice
    (4 options) -> look-elsewhere penalty stated, not hidden.
 E2 Reionisation: the gas regains partial contact (Compton/H rises again at z < 10). Expect Compton/H to stay BELOW 1 after
    reionisation. A smooth, reversible switch (extra mass proportional to contact) would put back ~a few % of delta at z = 0.89,
    far above the methanol bound 3.6e-7 -> EXCLUDED. A one-way (or threshold) handover is required.
 E3 The released energy must not go into gas or light (gas route ionises the universe: optical depth ~4 vs 0.054).
 E4 Fork with iteration 53: the size cannot come from both the cell tally and the electron payment. Distinguishing tests:
    electron route -> delta ~0.5% (SO/CMB-S4 precision) and the 21-cm step at ~11 MHz; tally route -> wrap-around space,
    delta unrelated to dark energy's size.
